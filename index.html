Our philosophy

The art of thoughtful beauty.

Beauty is personal. We begin by listening — to your preferences, your everyday life and the details that matter to you.

Precision gives our work its finish. A gentle approach gives the experience its meaning. Neither should come at the expense of the other.

We value thoughtful choices over passing trends, and a result that feels beautiful to you over one that simply asks to be noticed.

Made with intention. Worn with confidence.

Our story

A place to feel like yourself.

A studio experience shaped by the feeling it leaves behind.

Russian Shades was imagined around a simple idea: a beauty appointment can be something more than a service. It can be time that belongs entirely to you.

We wanted the experience to feel considered from the start — a warm welcome, room to settle in and artistry that respects your own sense of style.

That is the spirit behind our nail, lash and brow studio: expert attention to the little things, without losing sight of the person in the chair.

Every visit is a new moment, but our hope is always the same: that you leave feeling cared for, confident and unmistakably yourself.

The beauty is in how you feel.
